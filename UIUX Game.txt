Redesign the existing online Splendor-style anime board game UI into a premium desktop-first interface.

IMPORTANT:
- The game logic already works.
- Existing card assets already exist.
- Do NOT regenerate or replace card artwork.
- Focus on UI/UX, layout, responsiveness, interaction, readability and animation.
- Support 2, 3 and 4 players.
- Primary target: PC, especially 1920×1080.
- The full game board must fit on screen without vertical scrolling at 1920×1080.
- Do NOT globally scale the entire UI using transform: scale().

GOAL

Make the game feel like a polished premium PC digital board game, not a normal website.

Priorities:
1. Character artwork must be large and easy to see.
2. Card cost, prestige and permanent bonus must be readable instantly.
3. Nobles must be clearly visible and larger than before.
4. Player information must be compact.
5. Gameplay interactions must require as few clicks as possible.
6. UI should remain clean and balanced for 2, 3 and 4 players.

VISUAL STYLE

Use:
- dark navy / charcoal background
- subtle glass panels
- very light borders
- soft shadows
- restrained glow
- premium fantasy / luxury tabletop feeling
- bronze accent for Tier I
- silver accent for Tier II
- gold accent for Tier III
- platinum / diamond accent for Nobles

The anime artwork must remain the strongest visual element.

Avoid:
- large black opaque panels
- thick borders
- excessive blur
- excessive neon
- oversized labels
- unnecessary sidebars
- UI elements that cover character faces

On click:

- add a subtle selected ring
- animate with a small spring effect

Click again to deselect.

Show selection below:

Blue ×1
Black ×1

[Confirm]

The Confirm button remains disabled until the move is legal.

Do not open a modal just to take gems.

CARD HOVER

On hover:

scale card to approximately 1.10–1.15

translate upward slightly

increase shadow/elevation

Do not move neighboring cards.

Use approximately 160–200ms transitions.

Add very subtle 2–3 degree cursor-based parallax.

After a short delay, show a tooltip containing:

- character name
- tier
- prestige
- permanent bonus
- original cost
- actual cost after player's permanent bonuses

AFFORDABLE CARDS

Do not cover the card with a large green overlay.

Use only:

- subtle gold outline
- tiny check icon
- soft glow

Cards the player cannot afford should still show full artwork.

Only insufficient cost values should visually indicate that resources are missing.

CARD PURCHASE FLOW

Clicking a development card must NOT immediately purchase it.

After click:

1. Slightly darken the rest of the board.
2. Enlarge the selected card.
3. Open a compact purchase panel beside the card.

Panel must show:

BUY CARD

Cost after bonuses

Available player tokens

Gold joker tokens

Selected payment

Buttons:

AUTO PAY
CANCEL
BUY

The player must be allowed to manually choose which tokens are used.

Click individual tokens to select or remove them from payment.

Gold tokens can substitute missing colors.

AUTO PAY should choose a valid payment automatically.

BUY becomes active only when payment is valid.

PURCHASE ANIMATION

After Buy:

1. Paid tokens animate from player area to the gem bank.

2. Purchased card slightly shrinks and flies toward the player's permanent bonus area.

3. The permanent bonus counter increases with a short pulse animation.

4. A new development card enters the empty slot with a short fade/slide animation.

Total normal purchase animation:
approximately 500–700ms.

Do not use long cinematic animations during every turn.

RESERVE
CANCEL

Animate the card toward the local player's reserved area.

Animate Gold from the bank to the player.
